The Project

Premium urban lifestyle, in Toronto's core.

NORTHCORE at 53 Sheppard Avenue West brings together ground-level townhomes and a high-rise tower at one of North York's most connected intersections. More than a building — a well-rounded lifestyle of natural beauty, diverse community, and effortless transit.

Designed for urban professionals, move-up buyers, and discerning investors who want prestige without leaving the city. Restaurants, parks, and city energy are steps away; the subway is at your doorstep; and private rooftop terraces frame the full Toronto skyline.
