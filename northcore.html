The Townhomes

A four-storey house, in the core of the city.

The NORTHCORE townhomes at 53 Sheppard Avenue West are a small collection of executive 4-bedroom homes on one of North York's busiest corners. Built by Sevoy Developments, Fieldgate Urban and Westdale Properties, with architecture by IBI Group and interiors by Figure3.

The Location · Yonge & Sheppard

One of Toronto's most connected addresses.

The 53 Sheppard Ave W area scores 91 on Walk Score and 89 on Transit Score. That means two subway lines, the 401, and everyday errands all within reach on foot.

Two subway lines, one station

A short walk to Sheppard–Yonge Station, where TTC Line 1 (Yonge–University) meets Line 4 (Sheppard). The 401 is minutes away, and downtown is about a 30-minute ride.

Everything within a few blocks

Grocery, dining, cafés, banks, and fitness fill the Yonge & Sheppard corridor, with Bayview Village and Mel Lastman Square close by.

Parks & green space nearby

Neighbourhood parkland sits steps away, a quiet break from one of the city's busiest corners.

New for 2026

Ask about up to $130,000 in HST relief.

An eligible brand-new home can recover the full 13% HST (combined federal and provincial) under Ontario's enhanced 2026 rebate, for agreements signed between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027. The full amount applies up to $1 million and phases down above that. We'll confirm which NORTHCORE homes qualify and estimate your figure.

The enhanced 2026 HST rebate remains subject to legislation. Rebate amounts are maximums that scale with purchase price and depend on the signing window and your circumstances; homes above $1M receive less and the rebate phases to a $24,000 floor above $1.85M. For assignments, eligibility follows the original builder agreement date. Not tax advice; confirm with a tax/legal professional.
