New for 2026

Ask about up to $130,000 in HST relief.

An eligible brand-new home can recover the full 13% HST — combined federal and provincial — under Ontario's enhanced 2026 rebate, for agreements signed between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027. The full amount applies up to $1 million and phases down above that. We'll confirm which NORTHCORE homes qualify and estimate your figure.

Get My Rebate Estimate →

The enhanced 2026 HST rebate remains subject to legislation. Rebate amounts are maximums that scale with purchase price and depend on the signing window and your circumstances; homes above $1M receive less and the rebate phases to a $24,000 floor above $1.85M. For assignments, eligibility follows the original builder agreement date. Not tax advice — confirm with a tax/legal professional.
