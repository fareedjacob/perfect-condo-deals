How we collect, use, and protect your information, and your rights.

1. Who we are

“Perfect Condo Deals” is a real estate marketing brand operated under RE/MAX Hallmark, Brokerage (RECO Registration #[RECO #]) (“we,” “us,” “our”). All real estate services and transactions are completed by licensed Ontario real estate professionals acting on behalf of the brokerage.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a form on this website, we collect the information you provide: typically your name, phone number, email address, the project(s) you're interested in, your timeline, and intended use. We may also automatically collect limited technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and marketing identifiers like UTM parameters or click IDs) to understand how our site is found and used.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and send the deal sheets, price lists, floor plans, and details you request;
  • To contact you about pre-construction and assignment opportunities, pricing, and related real estate services where you have consented;
  • To match you with a licensed specialist who can assist with showings and purchases;
  • To improve our website and marketing.

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry and provide requested updates, in accordance with this policy and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). We do not sell your data.

4. CASL: electronic messages & consent

In accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), we send commercial electronic messages only with your consent. By checking the consent box on our forms, you expressly agree that Perfect Condo Deals / RE/MAX Hallmark, Brokerage may contact you by email, phone, or text about pre-construction and assignment opportunities, pricing, and related real estate services.

Your right to unsubscribe

You can withdraw your consent at any time. Every commercial email we send includes a working unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to text messages or email us at hello@perfectcondodeals.com. We honour unsubscribe requests within 10 business days. We retain records of consent (including date, time, and the consent wording) as required by law.

5. How we share information

We share your information only with the licensed real estate professional(s) who service your request and with trusted service providers (such as our CRM and email tools) that help us operate, under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information where required by law.

6. Data retention & security

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal and regulatory requirements, then dispose of it securely. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information; however, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Contact us at hello@perfectcondodeals.com.
