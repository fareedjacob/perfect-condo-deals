Good to Know

Common Questions

Is there any cost to me?

No. Our service is free to buyers — we're compensated by the builder when a deal closes. You pay the unit price, nothing extra to us.

Why aren't these on MLS?

Builder-surplus and assignment deals are typically sold privately, often with advertising restrictions. That's exactly why getting on our list matters.

Are the savings real?

Yes. We show you comparable resale data so you can verify the gap yourself. Market figures are estimates — we never guarantee future appreciation.

What's the 2026 HST rebate?

Every deal we offer qualifies for the 2026 HST/GST new-home rebate — money back in your pocket on closing. The exact amount depends on whether you're an owner-occupier or investor and the purchase price. We'll calculate your figure.

How much deposit do I need?

It varies by deal. Some — like 1515 Pickering — offer a monthly deposit program from $1,500/mo with as little as 5% down spread over the first year. We'll walk you through the full structure.

What happens after I fill the form?

You get the full deal sheet the same day. If you're interested, a licensed specialist books a private showing and handles everything through to closing.

Do I need to be an investor?

Not at all. Most buyers are end-users — first-timers and right-sizers — alongside investors. We match deals to what you're looking for.

How often do new deals come in?

Regularly. The GTA market is shifting fast right now, which means new builder-surplus inventory and assignments appear weekly.
